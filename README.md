# Awesome AI Video Agents [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of **AI video agents**: tools you talk to instead of operate. You describe the video, attach references, and the agent picks the model, the steps and the settings. Single-prompt text-to-video generators are out of scope unless they have an agent mode.

Each entry was checked against the vendor's own product page. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## What counts as an AI video agent

A tool belongs here if it does at least two of these:

1. Takes a goal in plain language and decides which model or tool to run.
2. Keeps context across turns, so the next request can build on the last result.
3. Runs more than one step (for example: image, then animation, then extension) in one conversation.

## Agents

- [HeyGen Video Agent](https://www.heygen.com/video-agent) - Turns a prompt or script into an edited video with an AI avatar, voiceover and motion graphics. Shows a scene-by-scene breakdown before rendering, and elements stay editable afterwards.
- [Pexo](https://pexo.ai/video-agent) - Starts from an idea, URL, PDF, image, video or audio and delivers a finished video with voiceover, music and subtitles. You revise by marking a moment and leaving a comment. Has a CLI and MCP integration.
- [Agent Opus](https://www.opus.pro/agent) - From the OpusClip team. Builds social story videos and ad variants from a script, voice recording or idea plus brand assets, with a library of viral formats to remix.
- [Pippit](https://www.pippit.ai/tools/video-agent) - ByteDance's creative agent powered by CapCut. Video agent, image agent and digital avatars in one product, aimed at e-commerce and TikTok Shop content.
- [iMideo AI Video Agent](https://imideo.io/ai-video-agent) - Chat-based workspace for making and refining visual clips from your own photos and videos: image to video, reference to video, video to video, extension, and face/head/body swap. Shows a confirmation card with model, settings and credit cost before anything runs. [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ayukx2YVlvc) (49s, real run).

## Comparison

| Agent | Best for | Inputs | How you revise | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HeyGen Video Agent | Avatar explainers, training | Prompt, script | Chat, then edit elements | Edited video with avatar and voice |
| Pexo | Launch videos, product explainers | Idea, URL, PDF, image, video, audio | Comment on a moment | Finished video with voice, music, subtitles |
| Agent Opus | Daily social content, ad variants | Idea, script, audio, brand assets | Remix templates, regenerate | Publish-ready short video |
| Pippit | E-commerce, TikTok marketing | Link, file, media, reference video | Keep chatting | Marketing video, images, avatar clips |
| iMideo AI Video Agent | Iterating on clips from your own media | Text, images, MP4 video | Chat on top of the last result | Generated images and clips, extended or swapped step by step |
